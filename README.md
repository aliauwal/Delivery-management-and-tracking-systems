# Delivery-management-and-tracking-systems
The Delivery Management and Tracking System is a web-based application designed to help businesses manage, organize, monitor, and track the delivery of goods or products to their customers.
The system provides a platform where business owners can register, create delivery requests, assign deliveries to available riders, and monitor the progress of each delivery. Riders can also register on the platform, receive assigned delivery requests, update delivery statuses, and confirm when goods have been successfully delivered.
